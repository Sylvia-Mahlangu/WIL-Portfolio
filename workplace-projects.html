01 · Workplace overview

Turning knowledge
into experience.

My placement gives me the opportunity to observe professional practices, learn from experienced colleagues and gradually develop my understanding of workplace technology.

As an ICT Application Development student at CPUT, I want to understand how the concepts I study relate to real working environments. My placement in the DENIS IT Department is an important opportunity to develop that understanding.

During my initial month, I took part in induction and training, became familiar with workplace systems and learned more about the environment in which the IT team operates. This helped me appreciate that reliable technology depends on understanding both the systems and the people who use them.

As I progressed, I began gradually assisting with system-related issues. I am also continuing to learn about networking. These experiences are helping me build my technical vocabulary, ask better questions and recognise the importance of patience, teamwork and following workplace procedures.
