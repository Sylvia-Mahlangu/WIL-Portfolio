My placement

Where I am and what I do

About DENIS

DENIS (Dental Information Systems) is a South African company that has managed dental benefits for medical schemes since 1996. It uses technology to help schemes handle dental claims, benefits and treatment authorisations for their members.

The dental teams run the dental business. The IT team keeps the technology behind it working.

The IT Department

It makes sure computers, software, networks and systems work properly so everyone can do their job.

  • Technical support
  • Networks and infrastructure
  • Cybersecurity and data protection
  • Systems, databases and backups

Network support

I help with network and connectivity issues and learn how computers connect to the company network.

Call-centre system support

I assist call-centre agents when their systems give them trouble, so they can keep helping callers.
