My placement

Where I am and what I do

About DENIS

DENIS (Dental Information Systems) is a South African company that has managed dental benefits for medical schemes since 1996. It uses technology to help schemes handle dental claims, benefits and treatment authorisations for their members.

The dental teams run the dental business. The IT team keeps the technology behind it working.

The IT Department

It makes sure computers, software, networks and systems work properly so everyone can do their job.

  • Technical support
  • Networks and infrastructure
  • Cybersecurity and data protection
  • Systems, databases and backups

Network support

I help with network and connectivity issues and learn how computers connect to the company network.

Call-centre system support

I assist call-centre agents when their systems give them trouble, so they can keep helping callers.

Logbook · Month 2: September 2026 · Practical exposure and IT support

Practical exposure and IT supportSeptember 2026
FocusDuring this month, the focus moved from observing to gradually assisting with system-related issues.
Activities and learning
  • Observing how IT-related problems are identified and reported.
  • Learning how to gather information before investigating an issue.
  • Gradually assisting with system-related problems under guidance.
  • Understanding the importance of communication between IT support and employees.
  • Learning why technical issues should be investigated carefully rather than solved through guesswork.
ReflectionSeptember helped me develop a better understanding of practical IT support. I became more familiar with the process of identifying problems, understanding their impact on users and seeking assistance when necessary. I also learned that not every technical problem has an immediate solution and that teamwork is important in resolving workplace issues.
