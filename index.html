04 / Personal reflection

Growth is a process.

Every experience offers an opportunity to learn, reflect and improve.

“The goal is not simply to gain experience, but to understand what each experience teaches me.”

My Work Integrated Learning journey is helping me connect my academic knowledge with the realities of a professional IT environment. Through induction, workplace learning, assisting with system-related issues and developing my networking knowledge, I am gradually building confidence and a stronger understanding of the field.

I recognise that professional growth requires curiosity, patience, communication and a willingness to learn from challenges. This portfolio provides a place to document that development and reflect honestly on my progress.
