01 · The story behind the student

More than a qualification.

My journey is about the knowledge I have gained, the experiences that have shaped me and the future I continue working towards.

My journey in technology is a story of growth, determination and choosing to keep moving forward. Completing my Higher Certificate in ICT in 2022 was an important milestone. It gave me a foundation to continue learning and to pursue my interest in the world of Information and Communication Technology.

I have continued investing in myself beyond my formal studies. Through a Data Analytics short course on Coursera, I explored another area of technology, while my online English teaching certificate gave me another opportunity to develop my skills. These experiences remind me that learning does not have to happen in only one way. Every new skill can contribute to the person and professional I am becoming.

My workplace experience as a CCA in the dental department has also been part of my development. Working in a professional environment has helped me appreciate responsibility, communication, teamwork and the importance of supporting the people who rely on a service. These lessons matter in technology too, because IT is not only about computers; it is also about the people who use them.

As an ICT Application Development student at CPUT, I am now bringing my studies closer to real workplace experience through my placement in the DENIS IT Department. During my first month, I received induction and training and began learning how workplace systems operate. In my second month, I started gradually assisting with system-related issues. I am also continuing to develop my understanding of networking.

I know that I still have much to learn, and I see that as part of the journey rather than a weakness. I want to understand the reasons behind technical problems, learn from experienced people, improve through practice and become more confident in what I do. My goal is to make every opportunity count and to keep building the knowledge I will need for my future career.

“I may still be growing, but I am no longer standing where I started.”
Sylvia Mahlangu · My personal reflection
