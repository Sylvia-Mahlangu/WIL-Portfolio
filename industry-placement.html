Chapter one · My story

More than a qualification.

My path into technology is a story of building knowledge, exploring new opportunities and staying committed to my personal development.

My interest in technology has grown into a serious commitment to learning, problem-solving and building a future in Information Technology. I began my formal ICT journey by completing a Higher Certificate in ICT in 2022. That achievement gave me a foundation to keep moving forward and to explore the wider opportunities available in the technology field.

I continued investing in my development through a Data Analytics short course on Coursera and an online English teaching certificate. These learning experiences have helped me appreciate the value of developing different skills, remaining open to new knowledge and challenging myself to grow beyond what I already know.

My experience working as a CCA in the dental department has also been part of my professional journey. It has given me experience in a workplace environment and helped me appreciate the importance of responsibility, communication, supporting others and learning how different roles contribute to a bigger service.

Today, as an ICT Application Development student at CPUT and an intern in the DENIS IT Department, I am continuing to bring my academic learning closer to real workplace practice. I am learning about workplace systems, becoming more familiar with technical support, assisting with system-related issues and developing my understanding of networking.

I am still learning, and I do not expect myself to know everything immediately. What matters to me is that I keep asking questions, improving my understanding, taking guidance seriously and making the most of the opportunities I receive.

“I am proud of how far I have come, but even more excited about what I can still learn and become.”

Sylvia Mahlangu · Personal reflection
