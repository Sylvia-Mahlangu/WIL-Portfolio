01 — The company

About DENIS.

A South African company managing dental benefits for medical schemes, using technology to keep it all running.

What they do

Helping medical schemes manage dental care.

DENIS (Dental Information Systems) is a South African company that has specialised in managing dental benefits for medical schemes since 1996. It uses technology to support dental benefit management.

Imagine a medical aid member visits a dentist for a filling. The scheme has rules about which treatments are covered and how much it will pay. DENIS helps administer the benefits and processes behind that, including claims and treatment authorisations.

The dental teams run the dental business. The IT team keeps the technology behind it working.

Dental claims

Processing information about dental treatments submitted for payment.

Dental benefits

Helping administer the benefits available under different medical schemes.

Authorisations

Assessing certain treatments before they are performed, per the scheme's rules.

Provider networks

Working with dentists and dental practices.

Technology systems

Supporting the digital systems used to manage these services.

02 — The department

The IT Department.

01

02

Keeping the network, internet connections and servers running so staff can reach the systems they need.

03

Cybersecurity

Protecting systems from unauthorised access, malware and data loss, vital with confidential member information.

04

Supporting business applications, maintaining data integrity and making sure information can be recovered.
